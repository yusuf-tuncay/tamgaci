# ABOUT TAMGACI
# ============================================
Tamgacı is a comprehensive AI-powered accounting and invoice management software designed specifically for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) and enterprises in Turkey. The application leverages advanced artificial intelligence and OCR (Optical Character Recognition) technology to automate invoice entry, document scanning, and entire accounting workflows, significantly reducing manual data entry and human error.
